Project notes / 01

React, TypeScript, Konva.js, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, YOLOv8

01

The annotation problem.

I wanted a tool where a model can suggest bounding boxes and a person can correct them without losing the edits. The starting workflow covers cars, pedestrians and traffic lights in driving images.

InputDriving images
ModelYOLOv8, with optional test-time augmentation
ReviewReact, TypeScript and Konva.js
PersistenceFastAPI, SQLAlchemy and SQLite
ExportJSON labels
Scope

An annotation workflow. Model output remains subject to human review.

02

From prediction to saved label.

I added zoom, drag, resize and delete controls to the canvas, plus a confidence filter. Test-time augmentation runs the model at several scales to look for small and distant objects. FastAPI persists the corrections through SQLAlchemy.

Design decision

The person reviewing the frame gets the final say.

What the evidence does and does not show.
